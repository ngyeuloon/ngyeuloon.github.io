01

Where it actually starts

In 2019, a volunteering project in my Foundation in Science semester took me to Kechara Soup Kitchen. I went once because the course asked me to. I've been going back weekly ever since.

What changed my mind was meeting the people. The story I'd absorbed — that someone ends up on the street because they're lazy, and all the other labels society hands to beggars and the homeless — didn't survive one shift. The real reasons were medical, financial, and structural, and none of them were visible from the outside.

The label on something is not the same as what's actually in it. That has turned out to describe most of my job.

How I got to data

Mathematics and science were always the subjects that made sense to me, so I took the Pure Science stream for SPM and carried on into a Foundation in Science built around physics and chemistry.

The plan at that point was psychology. Then, in the final semester of foundation, I hit the statistics module — and it was the first subject where I wanted to keep going after the assignment was submitted. I changed direction and read Business Analytics (Hons) instead.
