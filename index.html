Chapter 01

Fun Facts

A volunteering project during my Foundation in Science semester introduced me to Kechara Soup Kitchen in 2019. I have volunteered there weekly since.

It changed how I understood homelessness. I had assumed it came down largely to individual choice. Talking to the people we served made clear that the causes were more often medical, financial or structural — and that none of them were visible from the outside.

I took on the Registration Team Leader role, stepped away for a year, and returned to lead the Medical Team on the weekly distribution.

It taught me to check what sits underneath a surface explanation before accepting it. That habit turned out to be most of my work in data.
